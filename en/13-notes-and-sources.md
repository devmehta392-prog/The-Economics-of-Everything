# Notes & Sources

*Figures for revenue, store counts, and pricing cited throughout this book reflect publicly reported estimates at time of writing and should be re-verified before any commercial or academic use. The books below go deeper into each chapter's core ideas.*

**1 — Why Starbucks Costs So Much**
Dan Ariely, *Predictably Irrational* · Robert Cialdini, *Influence* · Ray Oldenburg, *The Great Good Place*

**2 — Why Your Netflix Bill Keeps Going Up**
Tien Tzuo, *Subscribed* · William Poundstone, *Priceless*

**3 — Why the Same Flight Ticket Has 200 Different Prices**
Robert Cross, *Revenue Management* · Richard Thaler, *Misbehaving*

**4 — Why an iPhone Costs ₹1,20,000**
Walter Isaacson, *Steve Jobs* · Geoffrey Parker, Marshall Van Alstyne & Sangeet Choudary, *Platform Revolution*

**5 — Why Virat Kohli Earns More Than a Doctor**
Sherwin Rosen, "The Economics of Superstars" (1981) · Michael Leeds & Peter von Allmen, *The Economics of Sports*

**6 — Why Zomato Delivers Your Biryani at ₹30**
David Evans & Richard Schmalensee, *Matchmakers* · Geoffrey Parker et al., *Platform Revolution*

**7 — Why Gold Prices Never Really Fall in India**
Niall Ferguson, *The Ascent of Money* · World Gold Council, annual India demand reports

**8 — How UPI Changed the Economics of Money**
Dave Birch, *The Pay Off* · NPCI & RBI public data on retail digital payments

**9 — Why Your Movie Ticket Costs ₹1,500 on Friday**
William Poundstone, *Priceless* · Robert Cross, *Revenue Management*

**10 — How HUL Makes Billions Selling ₹5 Sachets**
C.K. Prahalad, *The Fortune at the Bottom of the Pyramid*

**11 — How Instagram Created an Economy Worth Billions**
Tim Wu, *The Attention Merchants* · Nir Eyal, *Hooked*
