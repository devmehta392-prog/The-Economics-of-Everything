## 📘 About This Book

From Starbucks to Instagram, from Virat Kohli's IPL contract to a ₹5 shampoo sachet — *The Economics of Everything* takes eleven ordinary things and shows the pricing, psychology, and strategy hiding inside every one of them. No textbook graphs. No jargon left unexplained. Just the economics that's been running your life the whole time, finally made visible.

- **Free** — read the whole book online, no sign-up or paywall
- **Trilingual** — available in English, Hindi and Gujarati
- **Start anywhere** — every chapter stands on its own

## 💬 Feedback & Corrections

Spotted a typo, an outdated figure, or a phrase that reads awkwardly in Hindi or Gujarati? [Open an issue](https://github.com/devmehta392-prog/The-Economics-of-Everything/issues) or suggest an edit with the ✏️ pencil icon on any page. Corrections from native speakers are especially welcome.

**Repository layout:** each language has its own folder (`en/`, `hi/`, `gu/`) with identical file names, so `hi/05-virat-kohli.md` is the Hindi version of `en/05-virat-kohli.md`. Chapter header illustrations are shared in `assets/images/`.
